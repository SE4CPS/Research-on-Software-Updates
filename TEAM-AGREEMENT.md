# Team Agreement: Research and Development on Software Updates

This is the working agreement for everyone on the team (Research Assistants and
Independent Study students). We review it together in the first meeting of the
semester; you accept it by adding yourself to the **Acknowledged by** table at
the bottom (see [How to sign](#how-to-sign)). Changes to this agreement are made
by pull request, so the current version in `main` is always the one in force.

## Meetings

- **First week only:** we meet (virtually or in person) on **Monday, 2:00 to
  3:00 PM** to plan the week ahead.
- **Every week after that:** status review meeting on **Friday at 2:00 PM, in
  person**.
- If you cannot attend Fridays at 2:00 PM, propose **three alternative time
  slots** at the first Monday meeting.

## One central backlog

- All progress, tasks, and status are documented in **one central backlog
  only**: the GitHub Projects board **"Research and Development on Software
  Updates"** attached to this repository
  ([SE4CPS/Research-on-Software-Updates](https://github.com/SE4CPS/Research-on-Software-Updates)).
  Not email, not Teams. The board and this repository's issues ARE the backlog;
  there is no separate tool.
- Code lives in this repository, in your own topic folder.

## Everyone

1. **Share your GitHub handle** with Dr. Berhe at (or before) the first Monday
   meeting, so you can be added to the repository and the board. Your handle is
   also part of your signature in the **Acknowledged by** table below.
2. Each ticket has **one assignee** who is fully responsible for it: commit
   message, labels, type, and an effort estimate of **1 hour**.
3. Only start a ticket if all your previous tickets are **closed or blocked**.
4. Every ticket title starts with **`Week 1`, `Week 2`, ...**, matching the week
   the ticket was created.
5. Every week, create **1 additional 1-hour ticket** for: uploading code to
   GitHub, preparing the demo, and the weekly summary answering three
   questions: *What tickets did you complete? What tickets are you planning?
   Which tickets are blocked, and why?*
6. **Every Friday by 12:00 noon:** push your latest code to GitHub and update
   the status of every one of your tickets, before the 2:00 PM meeting.
7. If you cannot complete any item in this agreement, tell Dr. Berhe **by email
   (not Teams) as soon as possible**.

## Weekly ticket quota by role

| Role | Hours/week | Topic tickets/week | Plus the weekly summary ticket | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Research Assistant | 5 h | 3 tickets, each about (and at most) 1 h | 1 ticket, 1 h | 4 tickets |
| Independent Study (3 credits × 3 h) | 9 h | 7 tickets, each about (and at most) 1 h | 1 ticket, 1 h | 8 tickets |

## What NOT to worry about in week 1

These will be set up or explained together; do not let them block you:

- Access to GitHub or the code repository folder
- How to create or write a ticket
- Knowing your specific tasks or research direction
- Tools, frameworks, or languages

## How to sign

1. Read this whole agreement.
2. Raise questions or suggest changes as comments on the pull request that
   introduces (or amends) it; we resolve them in the Monday meeting.
3. Add one row to the table below with your name, your **GitHub handle**, your
   role, and the date, in a commit made from **your own GitHub account**. That
   commit is your acceptance of this agreement.
